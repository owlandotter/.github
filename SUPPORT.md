# Getting help

- Questions about the product, your account or billing: email [support@owlandotter.app](mailto:support@owlandotter.app).
- Joining the waitlist: [owlandotter.app](https://owlandotter.app).
- Bugs in a public repo: open an issue in that repo.
- Security problems: follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/owlandotter/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Please don't open a public issue.
